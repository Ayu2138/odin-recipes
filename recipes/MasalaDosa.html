Masala Dosa

Description

Veg fried rice is a popular dish made with cooked rice, fresh vegetables, and simple spices. It is quick to prepare and makes a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/2 cup chopped capsicum
  • 1/4 cup chopped beans
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped spring onions
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped vegetables and cook for a few minutes.
  3. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add the cooked rice and mix everything well.
  5. Cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  6. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
