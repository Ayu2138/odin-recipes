Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer butter masala is a popular Indian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based gravy with butter and spices.

It tastes delicious with naan, roti, or steamed rice. The gravy is mildly spiced and has a smooth, creamy texture.

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and tomatoes.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and cook the onion until soft.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook briefly.
  4. Add tomatoes and spices, then cook until the tomatoes soften.
  5. Blend the mixture into a smooth gravy after it cools slightly.
  6. Return the gravy to the pan and add water if needed.
  7. Add paneer cubes and simmer for 5 minutes.
  8. Stir in fresh cream and serve hot.
